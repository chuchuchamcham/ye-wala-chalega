This phone listens for the steady hum of a drone's propellers and reports it to the post.

Steady hum (tonal, like propellers)
Ground vibration
Strikes in the last 12 seconds

How it decides: lay the phone flat on the ground or a wall. It counts sharp knocks well above the background vibration, and reports when at least 5 come at a steady rhythm, between 0.4 and 4 seconds apart, as a shovel, pick or drill does. Footsteps and passing vehicles are usually irregular or continuous. This is a simple rule, not a trained classifier.

How it decides: a sound at least 12 dB above the room's background, with most of its energy in a few steady tones (as propellers make), held for 1.5 seconds. Wind, talking and claps are broadband or short and usually do not qualify. This is a simple stand-in for a real acoustic array, not a drone classifier.
